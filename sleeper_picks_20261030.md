# Halloween Cup sleeper hunt, Oct 30, 2026

Method: 85 Halloween-eligible species from the main account's box that were in neither the 18-mon wide-sweep pool nor the shadow panel. Per species, his best real copy by stat product (copies currently at or under 1500 CP; a few evolve-paths use the pre-evo's IVs), standard movesets, auto-fit level. Panel = 12 core mons x 0/1/2 shields = 36 duels (same panel as the shadow sweep). Teams screened vs the same 33-team meta, finals protocol identical to finals3.js (891 battles for the top 3). Scripts: sleeper_screen.js, sleeper_candidates.json, meta33.js.

## Verdict
No sleeper team beats Jellicent/Azumarill/Tinkaton (82.4%) or Tinkaton/Clodsire/Mimikyu (81.1%). The best sleeper, Dusclops, plugs into the same Azumarill + built-Tinkaton shell and lands ~5 points lower, so the Tinkaton build decision is unchanged.
