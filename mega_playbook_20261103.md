# Mega Edition playbook: Lead Tinkaton -> Clodsire -> Gastrodon (Nov 3, 2026)

Team: Tinkaton 10/14/15 (Fairy Wind / Gigaton Hammer / Bulldoze), Clodsire
0/14/13 (Poison Sting / Stone Edge / Earthquake), Gastrodon East Sea 0/10/15
(Mud Slap / Earth Power / Body Slam). Sims: 1v1 matrix + lead calls from
mega_playbook.js, per-team at 66 reps (both seats). W/L strings are 0/1/2
shields. Percentages rank against the hand-built 27-team meta, not ladder.

## Lead calls (Tinkaton lead)

STAY against: Mega Altaria, Mega Sableye, Mega Mawile, Mega Venusaur,
Mega Steelix, Mega Banette (loses only the 1-shield), Azumarill, Annihilape,
Carbink, Registeel, Bastiodon, Lanturn, Cresselia, Noctowl, Umbreon,
Charjabug (loses only the 1-shield), Lickitung, Mandibuzz, Aegislash
(needs shields: loses 0, wins 1/2), Tinkaton mirror.

SWAP:
- Mega Medicham lead: swap Clodsire (W/W/W). Gastrodon loses the 2-shield.
- Mega Lopunny lead: swap Clodsire or Gastrodon (both W/W/W).
- Shadow Feraligatr lead: swap Gastrodon and play the 0/1-shield game
  (G: W/W/L). Feraligatr is volatile into everything; do not commit 2 shields.
- Jellicent lead: the one genuinely bad lead. Tinkaton and Clodsire lose at
  every shield count; Gastrodon wins only at 2 shields. Swap Gastrodon and
  invest, the back line (their Clodsire, Azumarill) is covered by the others.
- Sableye lead: stay, but cap at 1 shield (Tinkaton W/W/L). No swap is better.

## Alignment traps (the whole point of the team)

- Gastrodon is a specialist, NOT a safe swap. It loses at every shield count
  to: Altaria, Lickitung, Cresselia, Mandibuzz, Noctowl, Mega Venusaur,
  Trevenant. If it lands on any of those, swap out immediately, even into a
  worse-looking matchup (the Bellossom lesson, Nov 3).
- Gastrodon's jobs: Azumarill, opposing Clodsire, G-Stunfisk, Charjabug,
  Steel walls (Bastiodon, Registeel, Mega Steelix, Mega Mawile).
- G-Stunfisk: Gastrodon is the ONLY answer (W/W/W). Tinkaton and Clodsire
  both lose to it at every shield count. Never leave Tinkaton in on it.
- Clodsire mirror: his Clodsire loses at every shield count (foe standard
  4/15/15 wins every CMP tie). Gastrodon takes opposing Clodsire, always.
- Trevenant: only Tinkaton touches it (W/W/L). Do not overinvest shields.
- Lickitung/Azumarill/Altaria stays the hard counter team. Line: Tinkaton
  stays and beats Lickitung, Clodsire takes Azumarill, Tinkaton or Clodsire
  take Altaria. Losses come from Gastrodon getting stuck on Lickitung or
  Altaria, both of which beat it at every shield count.

## Shield and closer rules (from the Nov 3 video autopsy)

1. Shield the nuke, not the bait. Seismitoad: no-shielding Earth Power then
   shielding Muddy Water cost a shield and ~40% of Gastrodon in one leg.
2. Do not spend the closer mid-game. Clodsire was the only Turtonator answer
   and got spent on Morpeko, which Tinkaton beats at every shield count.
   The endgame became Tinkaton vs full-HP Turtonator, the one losing
   alignment on the board.
3. A Pokemon dying with a shield in hand is a lost shield. Gastrodon died
   to Psychic Fangs at ~30% HP with a shield up.
4. If your Gastrodon is caught on Lickitung or a Grass type, swap instantly.
   Alignment losses, not straight-up losses, are how this team dies.
