# Fantasy Cup: Great League Edition — Team from the main account's Box

Cup rules: CP ≤ 1500, Dragon / Steel / Fairy types only. Runs Oct 20–27, 2026.
Roster source: 2026-10-16 export (4,399 Pokémon). Simmed on the PvPoke engine (battle3v3, same v2 setup as prior team sims) against a hand-built 24-team Fantasy Cup meta.

**Lead Excadrill → Altaria → Mimikyu — 80.0% (192/240), zero build cost**

| Slot | Pokémon | CP | IVs | Moves |

| 2 | Altaria | 1499 | 0/15/8 | Dragon Breath / Sky Attack / Flamethrower |
| 3 | Mimikyu | 1484 | 15/12/15 | Shadow Claw / Shadow Sneak / Play Rough |

All three are already in the box with exactly these movesets. No TMs, no unlocks, no power-ups. This was the best team in the full sweep (1,422 trios tested, then all 6 lead orders on the 12 finalists) and it is also the best zero-build team, so there is no upgrade to buy.

Why it works: Excadrill is the Ground/Steel hammer for the cup's Steel-heavy meta (Rock Slide covers Corviknight and Togekiss), Altaria walls the Galarian Stunfisk and Dragon lines, and Mimikyu cleans up Fairy and Ghost-weak back lines. It sweeps 18 of the 24 meta teams 10/10, including the standard Galarian Stunfisk / Doublade / Mimikyu core and both Melmetal shells except one.

## Notes on the big names

- **Galarian Stunfisk is the cup's marquee Pokémon, but not with his copies.** His four are attack-weighted (best 13/13/15 vs the rank-1-style 5/15/13 the meta standard uses), and the best one knows only Muddy Water. Even fully built (power up the 14/13/15, unlock, Rock Slide / Earthquake, about 102,500 dust), the best Stunfisk team hit 77.9%, and the cheap unlock-only version's best trio was 63.9%. Excadrill does the same Ground/Steel job better in his box.
- **Doublade:** he has exactly one (CP 1379, 14/12/15, Psycho Cut / Gyro Ball). Built (power up + Sacred Sword unlock, about 83,000 dust) its best team was 72.2%. Not worth it over the free team.
- **Melmetal, Orthworm, Togekiss: not owned at CP ≤ 1500.** His Melmetal is CP 2692, over the cap. Shadow Forretress, Shadow Steelix, and Shadow Excadrill are also not in the box.

## Method and assumptions

- Every number is a pooled win rate from the PvPoke battle engine (battle3v3, TrainingAI level 3, 2 shields each, perfect play both sides) against a 24-team Fantasy Cup meta built from the current guide/rankings core (Galarian Stunfisk, Doublade, Melmetal, Shadow Forretress/Steelix/Excadrill, Corviknight, Turtonator, Orthworm, Azumarill, Altaria, Tinkaton, Carbink, Mawile, Registeel, Togekiss, Galarian Weezing) using standard PvPoke movesets and rank-1 default IVs.
- Stage 1: all 1,422 legal trios from a 22-candidate pool, 72 battles each (102,384 battles). Stage 2: top 12 trios, all 6 lead orders, 240 battles each (17,280 battles).
- the main account's Pokémon were simmed at their exact box CP/IVs/moves, with "built" variants modeled only where labeled. Levels were derived by CP matching (the export's Level column is unreliable).
- The engine is near-deterministic: most matchups resolve 0/10 or 10/10 at 10 reps, so the win % is best read as "share of meta teams this team beats under perfect play," useful for ranking teams against each other. It is not a prediction of ladder win rate, and the hand-built meta (Stunfisk-heavy) shapes the absolute numbers.
