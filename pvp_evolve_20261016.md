READ-ONLY analysis of the fresh 2026-10-16 export (4,399 Pokemon). No game-account changes.

Scope: all 366 PvP-spread Pokemon (Attack ≤ 5, Defense ≥ 12, Stamina ≥ 12), classified by evolution stage using PvPoke gamemaster data.

## Summary

- **EVOLVE NOW:** 29 — base/middle forms whose final evolution is a strong GL/UL pick and whose evolved CP stays under the cap
- **DUPLICATES:** 11 species — keep one copy for Little Cup, evolve one copy to GL/UL
- **KEEP UNEVOLVED for LC:** 44 — LC-viable base forms (single copy, or sibling copy already evolving)
- **COSTUME — check before evolving:** 9
- **BORDERLINE (fringe finals):** 87 — evolve only if you want the niche
- **DON'T EVOLVE:** 14 — final not PvP-viable, middle form is better, or evolving exceeds the UL cap
- **NO PvP USE at current CP:** 45 — over LC cap with a non-viable final, or neither form is PvP-relevant

Key rule: a Pokemon already over 500 CP cannot be used in Little Cup (you can't power down). If its final form is GL/UL-viable, the only PvP use is evolving it. If the final form isn't viable either, it has no PvP use at its current CP.

## Caveats

- Evolved CPs are estimates (± ~10 CP) computed from derived level (CSV Level field is unreliable; level was derived from CP + IVs + base stats). Verify in-game before evolving anything near a cap (within ~50 CP of 1500 or 2500).
- PvP viability judgments use PvPoke GL/UL ranking tiers (YES = top ~100, MARGINAL = fringe ~100–200, NO = not ranked / not viable). Rankings shift with meta and move updates; borderline calls are judgment calls.
- LC viability judgments use the Little Cup meta (top ~100 LC). Fringe LC picks are marked as LC-viable but may not be top-tier.
- Candy costs are typical values (25 for first stage, 100 for second, 50 for single-stage, 400 for Magikarp/Swablu lines). Special items (Sinnoh Stone, Metal Coat, Sweet Apple, buddy requirements) are noted where applicable. Verify in-game.
- Movesets are not covered here. Several evolved forms need specific moves to be PvP-viable (e.g., Talonflame wants Incinerate + Brave Bird; Nidoqueen wants Poison Fang + Earth Power; Charizard wants Blast Burn). Check moves after evolving; some need Elite TMs.
- Shadow Pokemon keep Shadow status on evolution (Shadow bonus preserved). Costume Pokemon may lose the costume or be blocked from evolving — verify in-game.
- The 29 PvP-deletable copies from the dedup analysis are a separate list; some overlap with this analysis (e.g., Ponyta copies already flagged deletable). Evolve first, then re-check the deletable list, since evolving changes what you have.
