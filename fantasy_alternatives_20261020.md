# Fantasy Cup — Alternative Teams From the main account's Box (Not the Wigglytuff/Lucario/Turtonator Copy)

Same sim setup as before: PvPoke engine (battle3v3, perfect play both sides), 24-team hand-built Fantasy meta. Because single 240-battle runs moved ±2–4 points between replications, **every headline number below is pooled across 3–4 independent runs (720–960 battles per team)**. Win % = share of meta teams beaten under perfect play — good for ranking his options, not a ladder win-rate prediction. Roster source: 2026-10-16 export.

His current team, re-pooled the same way: **Lead Excadrill (hundo) → Altaria → Mimikyu = 79.6%**. Its bad shells: 0/10 vs Corviknight/Galarian Stunfisk/Tinkaton, Orthworm/Galarian Stunfisk/Mimikyu, Galarian Weezing/Galarian Stunfisk/Corviknight, Shadow Forretress/Turtonator/Azumarill, and 4.8/10 vs Mawile/Galarian Stunfisk/Doublade.

## 1. The Wigglytuff team (best of 165 Wigglytuff trios swept)

**Lead Turtonator (built) → Shadow Altaria (Fast TM) → Wigglytuff (Play Rough) — 78.1% pooled**

| Slot | Pokémon | CP | IVs | Moves | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Turtonator | 1496 | 15/12/11 | Incinerate / Overheat / Dragon Pulse | Power L20→L24 + 2nd-move unlock: **~99,000 dust + 83 candy** ⚠️ |
| 2 | Shadow Altaria | 1498 | 4/13/12 | Dragon Breath / Sky Attack / Flamethrower | 1 Fast TM (Peck → Dragon Breath), no dust |
| 3 | Wigglytuff | 1499 | 0/15/12 | Charm / Icy Wind / Play Rough | 1 Charged TM (Swift → Play Rough; may take a few, Elite guarantees), no dust |

Total: **~99,000 dust + 83 candy + 2 TMs.** Every competitive Wigglytuff team in the sweep needed Turtonator in front of her — Wigglytuff lead craters (15–20%) because Charm gets farmed by the Steel-heavy meta, and no non-Turtonator partner pairing broke 70%.

- **Who this beats:** it fixes four of his current team's five problem shells — Orthworm/Galarian Stunfisk/Mimikyu 10/10, Shadow Forretress/Turtonator/Azumarill 10/10, Mawile/Galarian Stunfisk/Doublade 10/10, Corviknight/Galarian Stunfisk/Tinkaton 6.7/10 (was 0/10).
- **Who this loses to:** 0/10 vs Turtonator/Doublade/Azumarill, Togekiss/Excadrill/Doublade, and (shared with every team here) Galarian Weezing/Galarian Stunfisk/Corviknight; 3/10 vs Melmetal/Tinkaton/Azumarill.
- **Head-to-head vs the counter-comp that beat him (Wigglytuff/Lucario/Turtonator, rank-1 builds): 10/10 in all three lead orders.** It is a perfect answer to that team.
- **Head-to-head vs Empoleon/Altaria shells: 0/10 in all six lines tested** (Empoleon or Altaria lead × Mimikyu/Doublade/Galarian Stunfisk third). This is the hard caveat — the anti-Steel Wigglytuff build is itself hard-countered by the other comp that beat him. Empoleon's Steel typing resists everything Wigglytuff and Shadow Altaria want to do, and nothing on this team punishes it.

**Verdict on the Wigglytuff team:** overall it's a hair *below* his current team (78.1% vs 79.6%, inside run variance — call it even) with the loss profile flipped. It is a targeted counter-pick, not an upgrade: devastating if his pocket is full of Lucario/Turtonator-lead counter-comps, a liability if Empoleon is around. (Cheaper Wigglytuff variant, same Turtonator cost, no Fast TM: Lead Turtonator → Wigglytuff → Excadrill hundo, 75.8% single run.)

## 2. Two other alternatives with different loss profiles

### Alt-A — the strongest team simmed, at a steep price ⚠️

**Lead Turtonator (built) → Skarmory (built) → Mimikyu — 84.8% pooled**

- Turtonator CP 1496 (15/12/11), Incinerate / Overheat / Dragon Pulse — ~99,000 dust + 83 candy
- Skarmory CP 1482 (0/12/12), Steel Wing / Sky Attack / Brave Bird — power L24→L27.5 + Sky Attack unlock: **~102,500 dust + 82 candy** ⚠️
- Mimikyu CP 1484 (15/12/15) — free, as-is
- **Total: ~201,500 dust + 165 candy.** The only team here that beats his current team by a clear margin (+5.2 pts pooled).
- **Who this beats:** sweeps all five of the current team's problem shells except Galarian Weezing/Galarian Stunfisk/Corviknight (0/10, shared by everything). Head-to-head vs the counter-comp: **10/10 in all three lead orders**, same as the Wigglytuff team.
- **Who this loses to:** 0/10 vs Turtonator/Doublade/Azumarill and the Galarian Weezing shell; 3/10 vs Melmetal/Tinkaton/Azumarill. Vs Empoleon/Altaria shells it's partial: 10/10 vs the Empoleon-lead/Mimikyu line, 6/10 vs Empoleon-lead/Doublade, 0/10 vs the other four lines.

### Alt-C — the free one

**Lead Shadow Dragonair → Mimikyu → Corviknight — 81.0% pooled, zero build cost**

- Shadow Dragonair CP 1494 (12/9/15), Dragon Breath / Body Slam / Aqua Tail — as-is
- Mimikyu CP 1484 (15/12/15) — as-is
- Corviknight CP 1497 (14/14/15), Sand Attack / Payback / Air Cutter — as-is
- **Who this beats:** all five of the current team's problem shells (10/10, 10/10, 10/10, 10/10, and 6.3/10 vs the Galarian Weezing shell that shuts everything else out). No Excadrill, no Altaria, no Turtonator — a genuinely different core.
- **Who this loses to:** the Doublade shells flip against it — 0/10 vs Doublade/Melmetal/Azumarill and Doublade/Steelix/Mimikyu (his current team goes ~8–10/10 there), 0/10 vs Carbink/Galarian Stunfisk/Altaria, 3.7/10 vs Shadow Excadrill/Altaria/Mimikyu.
- Head-to-head: vs Empoleon/Altaria it takes both Doublade-third lines 10/10 and the Altaria-lead/Galarian Stunfisk line 6/10, but loses both Mimikyu-third lines 0/10. Vs the counter-comp: 10/10 vs Turtonator lead, 6/10 vs Wigglytuff lead, **0/10 vs Lucario lead** — Lucario still deletes his lead, it just isn't a full-team shutout anymore.

*(Near-miss, for completeness: Lead Turtonator → Mimikyu → Altaria pooled 83.4% for the same ~99k Turtonator build, but it went 0/10 vs both the Lucario-lead and Wigglytuff-lead counter-comp orders — it fails the exact test these alternatives exist for, so it's not one of the two picks.)*

## 4. The pick

**If he rotates to ONE alternative: Alt-C — Lead Shadow Dragonair → Mimikyu → Corviknight.**

- It costs nothing. No dust, no TMs, no unlocks — all three are in the box with the right moves today.
- It out-scores his current team against the meta (81.0% vs 79.6% pooled) while fixing every one of the current team's bad meta shells, so it's a real rotation, not a sidegrade with the same holes.
- Against the two comps that beat him it at least splits the difference — it beats the Doublade-third Empoleon lines outright and the Turtonator-lead counter line — instead of being a guaranteed 0/10 into one of them the way the Wigglytuff team is into Empoleon.

When to pick differently:
- **Pocket full of Lucario/Turtonator-lead counter-comps aimed at Excadrill?** That's the Wigglytuff team's one job and it does it perfectly (10/10, all orders) — but it costs the ~99k Turtonator build and must be benched the moment Empoleon shows up.
- **Dust is no object and he wants the single strongest team?** Alt-A (84.8%) — it matches the Wigglytuff team's counter-comp sweep, keeps most of the Empoleon coverage, and tops the meta table. It is also the only option here that costs real resources (~201,500 dust), and it still loses to the same Galarian Weezing shell and Turtonator/Doublade/Azumarill as everything else.
