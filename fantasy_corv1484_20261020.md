# Fantasy Cup — Alt-C With the TM'd CP 1484 Corviknight (Verification Sim)

## Recommendation first

**Yes, TM the CP 1484 Corviknight — but spend both TMs.** Lead Shadow Dragonair → Mimikyu → Corviknight CP 1484 (1/15/14) with the full set match (Sand Attack / Payback / Air Cutter, 1 Fast TM + 1 Charged TM) pooled **83.3%**, beating the CP 1497 version's 81.0%. The Fast-TM-only variant (keeps Sky Attack) pooled 80.4% — no gain at all, don't bother with that one.

Caveat on the Charged TM: a regular one rolls randomly in Corviknight's charged pool, so landing Air Cutter over Sky Attack may take a few; an Elite Charged TM guarantees it. Sim only — nothing was changed on the game account.

## Numbers (same engine, same 24-team Fantasy meta, fixed lead order)

All figures pooled across independent 240-battle runs (10 reps × 24 meta teams), same method as the alternatives sweep.

| Version | Runs (wins/240) | Pooled | Run range |
|---|---|---|---|
| CP 1497 (14/14/15), Sand Attack / Payback / Air Cutter — the original Alt-C | 195, 192, 196 (720 battles) | **81.0%** (583/720) | 80.0–81.7% |
| CP 1484 (1/15/14), **Variant 1** — Sand Attack / Sky Attack / Payback (1 Fast TM) | 191, 192, 193, 196 (960 battles) | **80.4%** (772/960) | 79.6–81.7% |
| CP 1484 (1/15/14), **Variant 2** — Sand Attack / Payback / Air Cutter (1 Fast TM + 1 Charged TM) | 203, 197, 199, 201 (960 battles) | **83.3%** (800/960) | 82.1–84.6% |

Team otherwise identical: Shadow Dragonair CP 1494 (12/9/15, Dragon Breath / Body Slam / Aqua Tail) lead, Mimikyu CP 1484 (15/12/15, Shadow Claw / Shadow Sneak / Play Rough) second. The CP 1484 Corviknight sits at level 23, CP exactly 1484.

## Is it real or variance?

- **Variant 1 vs baseline: equal.** 80.4% vs 81.0% with fully overlapping run ranges — the better spread exactly cancels out losing Air Cutter for Sky Attack. No reason to spend just the Fast TM.
- **Variant 2 vs baseline: better, modestly.** +2.3 pts pooled, and all four Variant 2 runs (worst 82.1%) landed above all three baseline runs (best 81.7%). The ranges only just touch at the boundary, so call it a real but small edge, not a blowout — roughly one extra meta team flipped from a loss to a coin-flip/win region. Given it costs only TMs (no dust, no candy), it's worth it.

## Worst matchups (Variant 2, pooled of 40 battles per meta team)

The loss profile is unchanged from the CP 1497 version — same three hard shutouts:

- 0/40 vs Doublade / Melmetal / Azumarill
- 0/40 vs Doublade / Steelix / Mimikyu
- 0/40 vs Carbink / Galarian Stunfisk / Altaria
- 19/40 vs Shadow Excadrill / Altaria / Mimikyu
- 21/40 vs Shadow Steelix / Doublade / Azumarill

Everything else is a win or a sweep (18 of 24 meta teams at 38/40 or better). The TM'd 1484 doesn't fix Alt-C's Doublade-shell hole; it just performs a bit better across the matchups it already contests.
