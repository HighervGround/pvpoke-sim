## Why it works (1v1 coverage, 0/1/2 shields)

- Marowak beats all four 1v1 at 0 and 1 shields (only loses Mimikyu at 2 shields).
- Umbreon adds a second answer to G-Stunfisk, Melmetal, and Mimikyu (1-shield).
- Stunfisk adds G-Weezing and Melmetal coverage.
- Melmetal is the easy one: nearly his whole box beats it 1-shield. Mimikyu is the hard
  one: only Marowak, Umbreon, and Skarmory beat it 1v1 in his box.

## Holes (from the per-team breakdown, 10 reps each)

- **Mimikyu / Cresselia / Talonflame: 0/10.** Cresselia alone answers the whole team:
  Grass Knot hits both Ground types super-effectively, Moonblast hits Umbreon.
- **Melmetal / Cramorant / Pelipper: 4/10.** Double Water-bird spam vs two Ground types.

## Lead order is load-bearing

The same trio with Umbreon leading (Umbreon / Marowak / Clodsire variant) collapsed
to 40% vs threat teams. Marowak must lead.

## Comparison

- His 91% team (Mimikyu / Stunfisk / Umbreon) is still better on the general meta
  (90.9%) but only 66.7% vs these threat teams and 33% vs Mimikyu teams.
- If the goal is specifically beating these four, the Marowak team is the pick.
  If the goal is general ladder, the 91% team stays ahead.
